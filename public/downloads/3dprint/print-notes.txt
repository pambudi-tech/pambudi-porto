IVAN PAMBUDI CARD - 3D PRINT (Bambu Lab A1, 0.4 mm nozzle)

1. Print fit-test.3mf first (a few minutes). Put the plug's tabs into the
   coin's notches, push, and turn it clockwise: it should click and stop
   with its marker at the coin's marker. Too stiff or too loose: change
   CLEAR (now 0.15 mm) or DETENT (now 0.12 mm) in tools/build-card-3d.py
   and rebuild.
2. Print pambudi-card-3d.3mf: the card front up, the seal face up, as placed.
   PLA, supports off, 0.08 mm layers (0.08mm Extra Fine) for the crispest
   relief and the cleanest front over the pocket, 0.12 mm also fine. Card in
   white, seal in red (separate prints, or one plate with a filament each).
3. NFC: a round sticker tag up to 12.0 mm wide and 0.3 mm thick
   (e.g. NTAG213, 12 mm). Stick it in the recess in the end of the seal's peg.
4. On: tabs into the notches, push, turn clockwise about 40 degrees
   until it clicks and stops; the P stands upright.
   Off: turn it back anticlockwise past the click and lift.

Card     67.752 x 84.999 mm, the cut of card.pambudi.xyz; 1.4 mm thick
         (1.9 mm at the relief, raised 0.5 mm on the front, pressed into the back)
Pocket   0.8999999999999999 mm deep, 0.5 mm floor on the front: a 0.4 mm ledge with three
         notches, a 0.5 mm groove beneath it
Seal     19.0 mm wide, 2.303 mm tall; a 0.8599999999999999 mm peg with three
         0.38 mm tabs, 0.15 mm clearance; the tag's recess in its end
What the A1 shows: the pocket's top (the front's inside) bridges 15.7 mm;
the seal's rim overhangs its peg by 1.06-2.51 mm underneath, and its
underside eases off 0.15 mm towards the rim so a slight sag there can't lift it.
