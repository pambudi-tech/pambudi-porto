The card as it prints: 67.75 by 85 millimetres and 1 millimetre thick (2.26 over the embossed frame), its dimensions drawn round it while it is still, the frame and his name and links raised on the front, the back flat but for a round recess for a 25 millimetre NFC sticker behind the seal, and in the empty middle of the front a 19 millimetre wax seal with a P, 2.7 millimetres thick: set on two pins turned 45 degrees anticlockwise, then turned clockwise until it clicks, locked for good. Drag across it to turn it any way; it stays as you leave it, and a tap or default view brings it back face on; face on, a tap turns it over, as do Enter and the arrow keys. Mesh shows the file's triangles, without light or shade. Explode turns the seal back and lifts it off the pins; then a tap on the seal, or wax detail, shows it alone, and back to card, a tap beside it or Escape goes back. Pinch, Control or Command and scroll, or the plus and minus keys zoom in; 0 goes back to real size.
