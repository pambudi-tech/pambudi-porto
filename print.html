real size

Hold a bank card against the screen and slide until the outline matches its edges.

The card as it prints: 67.75 by 85 millimetres and 1 millimetre thick (1.5 over the embossed frame), its dimensions drawn round it while it is still, the frame raised on the front and pressed into the back, and a 19 millimetre wax seal with a P locked for good into a pocket at the middle of the back with a push and a turn, an NFC tag in its underside. At the top, card or key ring: the same card with a round tab on top and a 3 millimetre hole, 89.57 millimetres tall. Drag across it to turn it any way; it stays as you leave it, and a tap or default view brings it back face on; face on, a tap turns it over, as do Enter and the arrow keys. Mesh shows the file's triangles, without light or shade. Explode takes the seal back out of the pocket; then a tap on the seal, or wax detail, shows it alone, and back to card, a tap beside it or Escape goes back. Pinch, Control or Command and scroll, or the plus and minus keys zoom in; 0 goes back to real size.
